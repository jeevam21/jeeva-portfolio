About

"I once believed HR was only about payroll, attendance, and compliance."

Working in manufacturing and municipal water supply operations completely changed my perspective. On the factory floor, HR meant faces, shift patterns, and people who needed to be seen. In municipal infrastructure, it meant something else entirely — files, sanctions, measurement books, and a system that either worked or didn't, with no room in between.

Today, I build HR systems that improve operational efficiency through structured workflows, automation, and data-driven decision making. I've managed payroll for close to a hundred factory employees, and now coordinate HR and administrative documentation for field teams across four municipal zones — translating scattered daily activity into dashboards, registers, and reports that hold up to scrutiny.

The thread that connects both worlds: HR isn't just a support function. Done well, it's an operating system for the people who keep things running.

— Jeeva M

Project 02 · Automation

Sales Expense Automation System

A self-serve mileage and expense system for field sales teams, replacing paper claim forms with a validated digital flow.

Problem

Manual expense claims meant slow reconciliation, inconsistent mileage entries, and no photo proof of travel.

Solution

Google Forms intake with built-in mileage validation and image verification, feeding a Sheets-based dashboard reconciled monthly.
